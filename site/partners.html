Who can become a partner?

Coaches, course creators, Discord and Skool communities, agencies and creators whose audience runs or starts Shopify stores.

How do you know which stores are mine?

Stores that sign up through your link are linked to you automatically. If a student signed up without it, tell us and we link them by hand.

What is the commission?

A fixed amount per shipped order, agreed with you up front. It depends on what your students sell and how many ship. We tell you the exact number on a 15-minute call.

When do I get paid?

Monthly, for the orders that shipped that month. You see every store and every month in your partner dashboard.

Do my students pay more because of me?

No. They get the same all-in price as every other store. Your commission comes out of our margin.
