Which countries do you ship to?

We ship worldwide. Tell us where your customers are and your quote shows the route and delivery time per country.

Do I need to buy stock?

No. We buy per order, so you can start with zero stock. When a product sells steadily, bulk stock can lower your cost.

How fast do orders ship and arrive?

It depends on the product and the route. Your quote shows the expected processing and delivery time per country, and tracking is synced to your Shopify store.

What if a parcel is lost or damaged?

No delivery scan 30 days after shipping? Tell us and we reship it or credit the order price to your balance. Damaged or wrong item: send photos within 14 days of delivery.

I use AliExpress or CJ now. How do I switch?

Send your product links and we quote an all-in price. If we beat what you pay now on price or speed, we connect your Shopify and new orders run through us.

How does paying work?

You top up a balance and every order is paid from it. No contract and no subscription, so you can stop whenever you want.

How do VAT, duties and customs work?

Duties and VAT are prepaid wherever possible, so your customer never pays at the door.

What happens around Chinese New Year?

Factories close for about two weeks around Chinese New Year. We warn you weeks ahead, so you can stock up on bestsellers before the break.
